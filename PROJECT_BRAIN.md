## Financial Philosophy

The ERP is accounting-sensitive.

Every invoice must eventually affect:

* Dealer Balance
* Due Amount
* Ledger Entries

Every collection must eventually affect:

* Dealer Balance
* Ledger Entries

No orphan financial records allowed.
